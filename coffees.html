Featured Coffees

Espresso

A small, strong shot made by forcing hot water through finely ground beans.

Latte

Espresso with plenty of steamed milk and a thin layer of foam. Smooth and mild.

Cappuccino

Equal parts espresso, steamed milk, and foam for a light and airy texture.

Cold Brew

Coffee steeped in cold water for hours. Smooth, less bitter, and great over ice.
